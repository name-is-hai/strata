The XCursor artwork is unchanged. Identical aliases use relative symlinks.
Hyprcursor packaging uses the original 96px frames, animation delays and
click points, with format conversion only. This is raster artwork, not SVG.
Discrete sizes from 24 to 96px are pre-rendered with Cairo. Resizing is disabled
in Hyprcursor metadata to bypass its PNG downscaling clip bug. Other requested
sizes select the nearest complete frame. Original XCursor files are unchanged.
